# Initial Capture and Destruction Strategy Analysis

## Method

Technology-labelled publications were grouped into four operational
treatment strategies:

- `capture_only`
- `destruction_only`
- `capture_and_destruction`
- `unclassified`

The capture group includes:

- adsorption
- ion exchange
- membranes

The destruction group includes:

- photocatalysis
- electrochemical oxidation
- plasma
- hydrothermal treatment
- sonolysis
- biological treatment
- thermal treatment
- supercritical water oxidation

A publication was classified as `capture_and_destruction` when it
contained at least one capture label and one destruction label, or when
it received the explicit `capture_and_destroy` label.

Technology categories are not mutually exclusive. A publication may
therefore contribute to more than one capture–destruction technology
pair.

## Overall strategy distribution

The 658-record corpus contains:

| Treatment strategy | Records |
|---|---:|
| Capture only | 135 |
| Destruction only | 192 |
| Capture and destruction | 72 |
| Unclassified | 259 |

Across the full corpus, destruction-only records outnumber capture-only
records.

However, 259 records do not contain a technology label and therefore
cannot be assigned to one of the three treatment-strategy groups.

The full-corpus result should also be interpreted cautiously because
the `exclude_candidate` tier contains general treatment literature that
may receive technology labels without being substantively focused on
PFAS.

## Annual strategy activity

| Year | Capture only | Destruction only | Capture and destruction | Unclassified |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2018 | 2 | 0 | 2 | 10 |
| 2019 | 7 | 6 | 0 | 15 |
| 2020 | 13 | 20 | 8 | 31 |
| 2021 | 25 | 20 | 8 | 35 |
| 2022 | 19 | 30 | 11 | 36 |
| 2023 | 24 | 44 | 12 | 60 |
| 2024 | 23 | 33 | 12 | 43 |
| 2025 | 21 | 33 | 15 | 29 |

Destruction-only research overtook capture-only research from 2020
onward.

Destruction-only activity increased from six records in 2019 to 44 in
2023 and remained high at 33 records in both 2024 and 2025.

Capture-only activity increased more steadily, reaching a peak of 25
records in 2021 and remaining between 21 and 24 records annually from
2023 to 2025.

Combined capture-and-destruction publications increased from eight
records in 2020 to 15 in 2025, their highest annual count in the
analysed period.

Among technology-classified publications in 2025:

- capture only: 21
- destruction only: 33
- capture and destruction: 15

Combined approaches therefore represented approximately 21.7% of
technology-classified publications in 2025.

## Core-corpus interpretation

The core water-treatment corpus contains 253 records with at least one
technology classification.

Capture-only and destruction-only research are almost evenly represented
within this tier:

- capture only: 38.3%
- destruction only: 39.1%
- capture and destruction: 22.5%

This indicates that the apparent dominance of destruction-only research
in the complete corpus is not fully reproduced within the most relevant
water-treatment records.

In the core tier, capture and destruction are effectively balanced, while
more than one fifth of classified publications contain evidence of both
strategy types.

## Secondary and manual-review tiers

The secondary and manual-review tiers are more strongly weighted toward
destruction technologies.

Destruction-only publications represent:

- 61.1% of classified secondary records
- 63.6% of classified manual-review records

This may reflect studies involving:

- sludge and biosolids
- concentrated PFAS streams
- spent adsorbents or treatment media
- non-water matrices associated with water-treatment systems
- degradation studies where the treatment matrix is unclear

The manual-review tier should not be merged automatically into the final
analysis corpus. Individual records require screening before they are
used as evidence.

## Main findings

### Destruction research has expanded strongly

Destruction-only publications exceeded capture-only publications in every
year from 2020 to 2025 except 2021, when capture-only activity was higher.

The destruction category reached its highest annual count in 2023 and
remained above capture-only activity in 2024 and 2025.

### Capture technologies remain central

The results do not indicate that capture research is being replaced.

Capture-only publications remain numerous, and capture technologies also
appear frequently as components of combined systems.

Adsorption in particular remains important both as a standalone strategy
and as the leading capture component in combined publications.

### Combined strategies are increasingly visible

Combined publications reached their highest annual count in 2025.

Their 22.5% share of classified core records suggests that integrated
capture-and-destruction approaches are an important part of the research
landscape rather than a marginal category.

This may reflect increasing attention to complete treatment trains in
which PFAS are first concentrated and then destroyed.

## Interpretation constraints

The strategy classification is based on technology labels assigned by
keyword rules.

The presence of both capture and destruction labels does not prove that a
publication experimentally evaluated an integrated treatment train. Some
records may discuss multiple technologies independently.

The `capture_and_destroy` label is deliberately strict and contains very
few records. Most combined classifications therefore result from the
co-occurrence of separate capture and destruction labels.

The `exclude_candidate` tier contains many destruction-labelled records.
This demonstrates that technology labels alone are insufficient evidence
of PFAS water-treatment relevance.

Final conclusions should prioritize:

1. core records
2. manually approved secondary records
3. manually screened records from the review queue

Background and exclude-candidate records should not be used as primary
evidence.
