# Commercial Intelligence Coverage Gaps

## Purpose

This report identifies technologies that currently have limited organization coverage in the commercial-intelligence pilot.

Low coverage should be interpreted as a research gap in the current dataset, not as evidence that few organizations are commercially active in that technology.

## Research implication

Future organization discovery should prioritize technologies with single-organization coverage so that the pilot becomes less dependent on individual company case studies.

The objective is not to equalize coverage mechanically, but to distinguish genuine market concentration from incomplete research coverage.
