# Commercial Intelligence Index

## Overview

This section of the PFAS Water Technology Landscape extends the scientific and patent analysis with structured commercial and competitive-intelligence evidence.

The current pilot tracks organizations, technologies, commercialization models, deployment evidence and research-coverage gaps.

## How the reports fit together

The **commercial intelligence summary** provides the main cross-company interpretation.

The **organization comparison** and **organization profiles** provide organization-level evidence and commercialization context.

The **organization–technology network** and **technology commercialization summary** expose relationships between companies, technologies and commercialization models.

The **coverage-gap analysis** identifies technologies that are weakly represented in the current organization set.

The **research-priority report** converts those gaps into an evidence-driven shortlist for future organization discovery and commercial research.

## Current pilot scope

The current pilot includes five organizations and 21 structured commercial signals.

The commercial-intelligence layer is designed as an exploratory and reproducible framework rather than a quantitative market-ranking system.

## Next direction

The next phase should expand organization coverage in high-priority technology areas, then reassess coverage and research priorities as new evidence is added.

Longer-term development can introduce automated monitoring of deployments, contracts, partnerships, licensing, acquisitions, validation events and commercialization changes.
