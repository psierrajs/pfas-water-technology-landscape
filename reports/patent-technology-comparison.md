# Patent Technology Comparison

## Scope

This comparison integrates the included patent-family datasets for activated carbon and ion-exchange technologies.

## Family coverage

- activated carbon: 25 families
- ion exchange: 17 families

## Treatment-mode comparison

### Activated Carbon

- adsorption: 15
- capture and destroy: 1
- combined process: 6
- regeneration: 3

### Ion Exchange

- adsorption: 11
- capture and destroy: 2
- combined process: 1
- regeneration: 3

## Initial comparative signals

- Activated-carbon patents show a stronger presence of combined-process architectures.
- Ion-exchange patents show a relatively stronger emphasis on regeneration and capture-and-destroy concepts.
- Adsorption remains the dominant treatment mode for both technology groups.
- The comparison suggests that innovation is moving beyond simple contaminant capture toward regeneration, integration and destruction.
