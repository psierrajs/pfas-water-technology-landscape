# Initial Publication and Technology Trends

## Corpus

The analysis-ready corpus contains 658 records published between
2018 and 2026.

Records are assigned to the following operational tiers:

- `core`: direct PFAS water-treatment studies
- `secondary`: adjacent solid and residual matrices
- `manual_review`: treatment studies with an uncertain matrix
- `background`: contextual PFAS literature
- `exclude_candidate`: likely irrelevant records

## Publication growth

| Year | All records | Core records |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 2018 | 14 | 8 |
| 2019 | 28 | 17 |
| 2020 | 72 | 34 |
| 2021 | 88 | 41 |
| 2022 | 96 | 55 |
| 2023 | 140 | 67 |
| 2024 | 111 | 57 |
| 2025 | 98 | 64 |
| 2026 | 11 | 9 |

Scientific activity in the retrieved corpus expanded strongly from 2018
to 2023. Total records increased tenfold, from 14 to 140, while core
water-treatment records increased from 8 to 67.

The lower 2026 count should not be interpreted as a decline because the
year is incomplete. Recent years may also be affected by indexing delays.

Adsorption is the dominant technology category in the corpus. It has
more than twice as many labelled records as any other category.

Destructive technologies including photocatalysis, electrochemical
oxidation, plasma, hydrothermal treatment, sonolysis and supercritical
water oxidation also show substantial research activity.

## Interpretation constraints

Technology categories are not mutually exclusive. One publication may
receive several labels, so category totals should not be summed.

The strict `capture_and_destroy` label identifies only records using
strong integrated-treatment terminology. Other combined systems may be
represented through multiple technology labels instead.

The counts describe the retrieved and classified corpus rather than the
complete global PFAS literature.
