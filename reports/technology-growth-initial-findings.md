# Initial Technology Growth Analysis

## Method

Annual publication counts were calculated for each technology category
from 2018 to 2025.

The incomplete 2026 publication year was excluded from growth
comparisons.

Two three-year periods were compared:

- Early period: 2018–2020
- Recent period: 2023–2025

The recent-to-early ratio compares average annual publication activity
between these periods.

Technology categories are not mutually exclusive. A publication may
receive more than one technology label.

## Main findings

### Adsorption remains dominant

Adsorption is the largest technology category by a substantial margin.
It produced 91 labelled publications during 2023–2025 and reached its
highest annual count in 2025.

Its continuing growth indicates that capture technologies remain central
to PFAS water treatment research despite increasing interest in
destructive approaches.

### Hydrothermal treatment is accelerating

Hydrothermal publications increased from three records in 2018–2020 to
23 in 2023–2025.

The category reached a new peak of 13 records in 2025. This suggests a
recent increase in research activity, particularly around treatment of
concentrated PFAS streams, sludge and other residual matrices.

### Electrochemical oxidation shows sustained growth

Electrochemical oxidation increased from seven early-period records to
37 recent-period records.

Unlike some categories with a single temporary peak, activity remained
relatively high throughout 2023–2025.

### Biological treatment is growing from a small base

Biological treatment has the highest recent-to-early ratio, but this
result should be interpreted cautiously.

The category grew from only two records in 2018–2020 to 19 in 2023–2025,
so the large ratio partly reflects its very small starting base.

### Supercritical water oxidation is an emerging category

Supercritical water oxidation first appears in the classified corpus in
2021 and accumulated 14 records during 2023–2025.

Because the early-period count is zero, a conventional growth ratio
cannot be calculated. Its appearance and continued activity nevertheless
suggest that it is an emerging destructive technology worthy of closer
analysis.

### Photocatalysis may have passed a recent peak

Photocatalysis remains the second-largest category overall, but annual
activity declined from 16 records in 2022 to eight in 2025.

Further analysis is required to determine whether this represents a
genuine slowing of scientific interest, publication variability or
limitations of the retrieved corpus.

### Sonolysis and thermal activity weakened after 2023

Sonolysis peaked at eight records in 2023 and declined to four in 2025.

Thermal treatment also peaked in 2023 and declined from six records to
two by 2025.

These changes may indicate fluctuating research activity, but the annual
counts are relatively small and should not be overinterpreted.

## Interpretation constraints

The results describe the retrieved OpenAlex corpus rather than the
complete global literature.

Technology categories overlap, and individual publications may
contribute to several categories.

Growth ratios based on small early-period counts can appear very large.
Absolute recent publication volume should therefore be considered
alongside relative growth.

The strict `capture_and_destroy` category contains only two records and
is not sufficiently populated for meaningful trend analysis.
